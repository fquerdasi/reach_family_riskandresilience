# reach_family_riskandresilience
Analysis scripts (Mplus and R) for a paper examining patterns of family risk and resilience factors to early childhood vulnerability to intergenerational transmission of abdominal pain. 

**Citation**:

Francesca R. Querdasi, Caitlin Murray, Peyton Johnson, Tasha B. Murphy, Rona L. Levy, Miranda A.L. van Tilburg, Margaret R. Kuklinski, Jennifer A. Bailey, Waylon Howard, Hafsah Diakhate, Kristen Daniels, Isabel Badillo, Tonya M. Palermo,
Family risk and resilience patterns differentiate early childhood vulnerability to intergenerational transmission of abdominal pain,
The Journal of Pain,
Volume 52,
2027,
106482,
ISSN 1526-5900,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpain.2026.106482.
